Graduate Researcher, KAIST Agentic RAG and LLM Recommendation Advised by Prof. Mun Yong Yi.
  • Developed AgenticTableRAG (ATR) for hybrid table-text question answering, combining sub-query routing and evidence verification to achieve higher F1 than all evaluated baselines across three benchmarks under matched retrieval conditions.
  • Distilled the routing policy into a 66M-parameter student that matched 98.6% of teacher routing decisions on HybridQA, eliminated per-sub-query LLM calls for route selection, and transferred to unseen benchmarks.
